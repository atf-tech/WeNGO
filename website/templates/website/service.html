What is WeNGO does ?.

WeNGO is a women empowerment NGO committed to uplifting women and supporting underprivileged communities. Through education support, food assistance, healthcare help, and social welfare initiatives, we strive to create opportunities and bring positive change. With the support of compassionate donors and volunteers, we work to build a stronger and more hopeful future for those in need.
