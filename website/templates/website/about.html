About WeNGO

Building Strong Women, Building Strong Communities

Women Empowerment NGO (WENGO) was established with the vision of building a strong, self-reliant, and inclusive society. Our organization focuses on empowering women, supporting children’s education, improving healthcare access, and uplifting marginalized communities.

Through continuous social service activities, WENGO strives to bring hope, dignity, and sustainable change to the lives of those in need.

Read More About Our Mission. Vission. Goal.

To build an inclusive and compassionate society by empowering women, educating children, supporting the needy, and promoting dignity, care, and equal opportunities for all.

  • WeNGO Vision.

    To create a society where women are empowered, children are educated, and every individual lives with dignity and equal opportunity.

  • WeNGO Mission.

    • Empower women through education, skills, and livelihoods
    • Support vulnerable children with education and nutrition
    • Provide healthcare and emergency support to the needy
    • Promote dignity, inclusion, and compassion for all

Become a Volunteer

At Women Empowerment NGO (WENGO), we believe real change begins with people who care. As a volunteer, you can stand with women, children, and marginalized communities, helping them move towards a life of dignity, opportunity, and self-reliance.

Together, we can build a stronger and more compassionate society.
