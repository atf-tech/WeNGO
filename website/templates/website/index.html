Our Focus Areas:

We empower women through education, vocational training, and livelihood programs to help them become self-reliant and confident.

We support underprivileged children with access to education, learning materials, and a better future.

We provide medical support, health awareness, and emergency assistance to individuals in need.

We ensure proper nutrition by providing food support to children, women, and vulnerable communities.

We work to uplift differently abled individuals by providing care, support, and opportunities for a dignified life.

We assist homeless and destitute individuals with basic needs, care, and rehabilitation support.

We promote environmental protection and compassion towards animals through awareness and welfare initiatives.

  • WeNGO Vision.

    To create a society where women are empowered, children are educated, and every individual lives with dignity and equal opportunity.

  • WeNGO Mission.

    • Empower women through education, skills, and livelihoods
    • Support vulnerable children with education and nutrition
    • Provide healthcare and emergency support to the needy
